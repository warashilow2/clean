What's included in a deep clean

Some of the points from the checklist our cleaners work from and tick off as they go. Extras can be added when we agree your price.

General areas
  • Dusting throughout: surfaces, shelves, light switches and handles
  • Floors and carpets vacuumed, hard floors mopped in every room
  • Skirting boards, door frames, radiators and light fittings washed
  • Behind and under furniture and appliances, heavier pieces moved
  • and the rest of the list for this room
Kitchen
  • Worktops, sink and taps, hob and splashback
  • Cupboard fronts and the outside of appliances
  • Extractor hood degreased, tops of all cupboards washed
  • Floor washed and the bin cleaned
  • and the rest of the list for this room
Bathrooms
  • Toilet, shower, bath, sink and taps
  • Limescale off taps, shower screens and tiles
  • Grout scrubbed rather than wiped
  • Mirrors polished
  • and the rest of the list for this room
Bedrooms & living rooms
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Tops of all wardrobes and cupboards
  • Inside wardrobes and room cupboards dusted

Frequently asked questions

What if I am not home?

Most clients leave a key or a door code with us, or a neighbour lets the team in. Tell us what suits when you book and we work around it.

Do you mind pets?

Not at all. Let us know what you have so the team arrives ready, and say if a room needs to stay shut while we work.

What do you clean with?

Certified eco products, safe around children and pets, and we bring everything with us. If you would rather we used something of your own, leave it out and we will.

Can I move or cancel a visit?

Yes. Give us as much notice as you can and we will find another slot that works for you.

Where do you cover?

Dublin city and county. If you are just outside it, ask anyway and we will tell you straight away whether we can get to you.

When can I reach you?

Every day, 8am to 11pm. Leave your number and we call you back within the hour.

Ready for a cleaner home?
