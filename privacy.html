Your details, in plain English

We ask for two things: your name and your phone number. This page says what happens to them. It is short because there is not much to say.

Who we are

Eco Spark Cleaning, a cleaning company working in Dublin and Co. Dublin. We are the data controller for everything described here.

  • Phone: +353 87 483 8743
  • Email: eco.spark.cleaning.ie@gmail.com

What we collect, and when

Only when you send us a request through the cost calculator. Reading the site collects nothing by itself - and nothing at all unless you agreed to the cookies described further down.

  • Your name and phone number, because we have to be able to call you back.
  • The request itself: the kind of clean, the number of rooms, any extras you ticked, and the price the calculator worked out.

We do not ask for your email, your address or your payment details on this site, and the form has nowhere to put them.

Why we are allowed to hold it

You asked us for a price. Under the GDPR that is Article 6(1)(b): taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We are not relying on consent for it, which also means you cannot be asked to agree to anything extra in order to get a quote.

We do not use your number for marketing, we do not add you to a list, and we do not sell or share it with anyone trying to sell you something.

Who else sees it

  • Our own team, to call you back and do the work.
  • n8n, the automation service that carries the request from the website to us, and Google Workspace, where we keep the record of it. Both act on our instructions only.
  • Google and Meta, but only if you agreed to cookies, and only for the counting described below. They never receive your name or your phone number - those are not on the pages their scripts can read, and we do not pass them on.

If you contact us through WhatsApp instead, that conversation happens on WhatsApp and their own terms apply to it.

How long we keep it

  • If the quote comes to nothing: up to 12 months, then it is deleted.
  • If you become a customer: 6 years, because Irish tax law requires us to keep business records that long.

Ask us to delete it sooner and we will, unless that second rule applies.

Cookies

We ask before we set any. Until you press Accept on the bar at the bottom of the page, nothing is stored on your device and nothing is sent to Google or Meta - not even your IP address, because their scripts are not loaded at all until then. Press "Only what is needed" and they never load.

If you do agree, these are set:

  • _ga and _ga_EGYSK3HKDK, by Google Analytics, to count visits and see which parts of the site people use. Two years.
  • _fbp, by Meta, so we can tell whether our ads brought someone here. Three months.

One more thing is stored either way, and it is the record of your answer to this question, so that you are not asked again on every page. It stays in your browser and we never see it.

You can change your mind at any time - the "Cookies" link at the bottom of every page brings the choice back. Withdrawing is meant to be as easy as agreeing was.

Google and Meta are both based in the United States. Where your data goes to them, it is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has ruled gives protection equivalent to the GDPR.

What you can ask us to do

You have the right to:

  • see what we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it deleted;
  • have us stop or limit what we do with it;
  • object to us holding it at all;
  • get a copy in a portable form.

Email or ring us and we will deal with it within one month. There is no charge.
