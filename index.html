Ask us anything
What if I am not home?

Most clients leave a key or a door code with us, or a neighbour lets the team in. Tell us what suits when you book and we work around it.

Do you mind pets?

Not at all. Let us know what you have so the team arrives ready, and say if a room needs to stay shut while we work.

What do you clean with?

Eco-certified products, safe around children and pets, and we bring everything with us. If you would rather we used something of your own, leave it out and we will.

Can I move or cancel a visit?

Yes. Give us as much notice as you can and we will find another slot that works for you.

Where do you cover?

Dublin city and county. If you are just outside it, ask anyway and we will tell you straight away whether we can get to you.
